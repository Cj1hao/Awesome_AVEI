Open research signals

Four directions for reliable embodied audio-visual intelligence.

  1. 01
    Hallucination and evidence-grounded decisions

    Link consequential conclusions to sensory intervals, entities, timestamps, and coordinate frames, with uncertainty governing further observation or clarification.

  2. 02
    Action-conditioned world models and active sensing

    Predict how interventions alter environmental states and sensory evidence, then select observations by decision benefit relative to time, energy, and interaction cost.

  3. 03
    Streaming fusion, memory, and real-time control

    Retain transient events, revise ongoing actions, and measure latency, recovery, and memory cost under strictly causal access.

  4. 04
    Scalable data and sim-to-real transfer

    Vary objects, rooms, sensors, and embodiments while preserving action-relevant physics and reporting calibration, failures, and corrective behavior.
