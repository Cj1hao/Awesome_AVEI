Open research signals

Six directions for reliable embodied audio-visual intelligence.

  1. 01
    Evidence-grounded decisions

    Preserve provenance and uncertainty from observation through action.

  2. 02
    Action-conditioned world models

    Predict how interventions alter both the world and its multisensory evidence.

  3. 03
    Streaming fusion and memory

    Maintain asynchronous audio-visual context under real-time control budgets.

  4. 04
    Physically consistent sim-to-real

    Model geometry, materials, reverberation, and device variation together.

  5. 05
    Generalist AV-language-action policies

    Use sound as actionable evidence rather than an auxiliary feature.

  6. 06
    Closed-loop evaluation

    Connect localization and reasoning gains to action, recovery, and latency.
